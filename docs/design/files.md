# 3DCraft design: Files

How documents and results reach files: the native format (`craft3d-format` over `craft3d-zipstore`), STEP (`craft3d-step` + `io::step`), meshes (`craft3d-meshio` + `io`), flat outlines (`craft3d-drawing` + `io::drawing`). §8 of the map ([`../architecture.md`](../architecture.md)); the formats themselves are specified in [`../file-formats.md`](../file-formats.md).

## Method

**Standalone format crates, one mapping crate.** `step`, `meshio`, `drawing` and `zipstore` each have a model of their own and depend on no workspace crate (`meshio` uses `zipstore` for 3MF); `io` maps bodies, meshes and outlines onto them. A format's quirks never reach the core types.

**`.3dcraft`** is a stored ZIP holding a pretty-printed `manifest.json`: the document as the user wrote it (the history, the variables and tables, the sketches with their last solution, appearance and material, assertions, named sets, public parameters and document info, hidden features), at `format_version` 35 (0.1.0, 2026-10-23), older versions migrated on load and every released version's file held by a frozen fixture ([`../file-formats.md`](../file-formats.md) › *The promise*). Beside the manifest: `blobs/<sha256>.step`, the bytes of each imported STEP file (format 25), and `thumb.png`, a picture that loading ignores (format 32; written for a Jig). A published Jig, `.3dcraftjig`, is the same format under its own extension, opened as a Jig in use (`crates/format/src/lib.rs` › `JIG_EXTENSION`). The file's types are separate from `doc`'s and converted by exhaustive destructuring, so a new document field does not compile until it is saved and loaded. Saves are atomic and refuse an invalid document; loads validate, and the reader is hardened against hostile archives. No body is stored: a file regenerates to the bodies it was saved from. The same writer serves autosave and crash recovery ([`engine.md`](engine.md)).

**STEP** export writes ISO 10303-21 with AP214 B-rep entities (manifold solids with voids, advanced faces, every curve and surface kind including rational B-splines), the document's units and uncertainty, and colours per solid and per face, after checking each body. Import reads the solids and sheets of AP203, AP214 and AP242 files into an `import` feature ([`step-import.md`](step-import.md)).

**Meshes**: binary and ASCII STL, 3MF (with base materials per object and per triangle) and OBJ (with a `.mtl` for colours), from the tessellator at a `quality` (coarse, normal, fine: a distance from the model's size and an angle), a tolerance asked for, or the automatic one; `io` welds the mesh and classifies it as solid or surface. **Flat outlines**: DXF and SVG of a sketch, of flat faces (several on one sheet) or of the flat faces of a named set, every curve as what it is, at true size. **Projected views** (`engine/src/view_export.rs`, 2026-10-23): with no sketch, face or set named, DXF and SVG draw the model as seen from a view, its edges and the silhouettes of curved faces split where they go behind a face, hidden parts left out or dashed; curves are polylines of the mesh within the tolerance ([`../file-formats.md`](../file-formats.md) › *Projected views*).

**Held to** by round trips, malformed-input sweeps, and FreeCAD reading the exports back as valid solids of our volume and area (`cargo xtask freecad`).

## Findings and history

> Moved verbatim from `docs/architecture.md` on 2026-10-15. Section numbers (§4.1, §5.3, …) in the text are those of that file before the split; [README.md](README.md) says where each one is now.

### From *Current state (2026-10-12)*

- **Mesh export:** `meshio` writes binary and ASCII STL, 3MF and OBJ; `io` welds a tessellated mesh, classifies it as solid or surface and encodes one or more objects. FreeCAD reads the box, the teapot and all fourteen tessellated reference solids (primitives, hemisphere, and the solids with trimmed faces) back as closed solids with the same counts, area and volume (`cargo xtask freecad`). No readers; colours in 3MF since 2026-10-15.
- **Native format:** `format` reads and writes `.3dcraft`: a stored ZIP (`zipstore`, with a reader hardened against hostile archives) holding a pretty-printed `manifest.json`, now at `format_version` 4 (version 1 to 3 files still open, through the migration chain). The file's types are separate from `doc`'s and converted by exhaustive destructuring, so a new document field does not compile until it is saved and loaded (verified by adding one). Saves are atomic and refuse an invalid document; loads validate. Not built: `thumb.png`, `blobs/`, the directory layout (autosave and crash recovery were built on 2026-10-13: [`engine.md`](engine.md)).
- **STEP export (2026-10-13):** `step` reads and writes ISO 10303-21 with its own model (records with parameters, simple and complex instances; nothing about CAD in it); `io::step` maps bodies to the AP214 B-rep entities (`MANIFOLD_SOLID_BREP`, `BREP_WITH_VOIDS`, `ADVANCED_FACE`, every curve and surface kind incl. rational B-splines, vertex loops for whole spheres and tori and for an apex inside a face) with the document's units, uncertainty and names, after checking each body; `file.export` and `craft3d-cli convert` write `.step`/`.stp`. For that `io` now depends on `geom`, `topo` and `kernel` (the latter only for `boolean::contains_points`, to tell which lump a cavity is in). FreeCAD reads 448 exported files as valid solids of our volume and area (`cargo xtask freecad`). No import yet, no assemblies or names of faces; colours per solid and per face since 2026-10-15. The conventions are in [`file-formats.md`](../file-formats.md) and in the module documentation.
- **DXF and SVG export (2026-10-13):** `drawing` writes ASCII DXF (AutoCAD 2000) and SVG 1.1 from its own 2D model (chains of lines, circular and elliptic arcs and B-splines; whole circles and ellipses; a unit; layers); `io::drawing` maps a sketch (its curves joined into chains) and the loops of flat faces (seen from outside the body) onto it; `file.export` writes them for a `sketch` or a `face`. Every curve stays what it is. FreeCAD reads both formats back with the closed-form length and area ([`file-formats.md`](../file-formats.md) › Flat outlines).
- **Flat faces for laser cutting (2026-10-17, laser-box findings L4 and L5):** (1) `file.export {face}` resolves the name as a feature resolves a reference that follows a body (`regen::follow_face`, the public face of `follow::face`, and `newest_body` for `feature`): a blank's `F1:face(1)` reaches the panel the cuts made of it; no rule of its own. (2) The drawing frame is no longer the plane's own frame, which depended on how the operation built the plane (a block's bottom came out with x along y): `io::face_frame` takes the outward normal and puts y up the face (model +z projected), or on a face lying flat x along model +x, origin the model's origin dropped onto the plane; `FaceAxes::Along` takes a given x; `FaceAxes::Own` keeps the old frame for `face_outline`. Every frame is right-handed with z out of the material, so an outline is never mirrored: the tests read the SVG back and check that every outer boundary has a positive signed area for the six faces of an upright block and of a block leaning about two axes, and holes that are chains a negative one (`drawing::Path::area`, Green's theorem, exact for lines, arcs and ellipses). (3) `file.export {faces}` lays several faces out on one sheet: each its own layer, a shelf layout by bounding box in the order given (`gap`, `width`), no nesting. `Path::translated` moves an outline. Tests: `crates/engine/tests/flat_sheet.rs` (followed names, pattern copies listed, a kept mirror, the frame in all directions, `xAxis`, `origin: corner`, the laser-box panels on a sheet by their blanks' names, rows, layer names, byte-identical twice, 30 refusals), `crates/io/tests/drawing.rs` (the frame rule), `crates/drawing` (area, translation), FreeCAD reading a sheet (`drawing_freecad.rs`).


### 9. Files

Detail in [`file-formats.md`](../file-formats.md).

- **Native `.3dcraft`** (`format`): a stored ZIP with a versioned `manifest.json` holding the document. *(Planned: the same layout as a directory, and content-addressed blobs for imported bodies and optional regeneration caches.)* Atomic saves, autosave and crash recovery through the same writer.
- **STEP** (`step` + `io`): export of exact B-rep (AP214 geometry and topology) is built; import later. STEP carries the result, not the history.
- **Meshes** (`meshio` + `io`): STL, 3MF and OBJ export from `tess` output at a caller-chosen tolerance.
- **Flat outlines** (`drawing` + `io`, 2026-10-13): DXF and SVG export of a sketch or of a flat face, as true curves at true size, for laser cutting.
