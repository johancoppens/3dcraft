# Licence of the test data in this folder

Three published files of the 3DCraft Jigs collection,
[johancoppens/3dcraft-jigs](https://github.com/johancoppens/3dcraft-jigs), kept as test data. Until
2026-11-10 they were start-screen examples in `docs/examples/published`, copied unchanged from that
repository's published files at commit `186a110fff8f5490943b6c373b589ca6f100fa65`; they are no longer
built into the program. Tests of the engine, the window and the web build open them (an assembly whose
motion plays, a Jig on its parameters). They are not edited here.
